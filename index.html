About this map

An unofficial, free fan project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Amsterdam Dance Event. For official information, tickets and last-minute changes, always use amsterdam-dance-event.nl. Event data comes from the public ADE program, last updated 27 Oct 2026, 09:05, refreshed twice a day, including which events are sold out. Sold-out events link to a search on TicketSwap, which ADE names as its marketplace partner; check listings there yourself. Times and lineups change, and some events sell out, so always check the event page before you go. ADE publishes no prices, so paid events show none.

ADE and Amsterdam Dance Event are names of their owner, used here only to say which event this map covers. Everything is provided as is: listings come from public sources and can be wrong, change or be cancelled, and organisers and venues decide on entry. Your saved plan stays in your browser. Visit counts are anonymous and cookieless (Umami, hits.sh); hits.sh sees your IP address when it shows the counter.

Late events starting after midnight are listed under the night they belong to (a 02:00 start on Thursday shows under Wednesday, marked Thu).

Buildings: Amsterdam, building by building by Alex Barakov (MIT licence), from 3D BAG by TU Delft (CC BY 4.0), BAG and BGT (CC0) and Gemeente Amsterdam open data. Addresses geocoded with PDOK. Rendering by three.js.

Visits are counted anonymously with Umami and hits.sh, with no cookies. Made by RadTech. Spotted a wrong venue or time? Tell us at radtech.nl. Enjoying it? Buy us a drink.
