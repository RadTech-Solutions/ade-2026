ADE 2026Amsterdam Dance Event turns 30. The free side, day by day.

Every ADE festival, Arts & Culture and Friends event from the public ADE program, with the 192 free ones marked and filtered on by default. Each venue is placed in its district, and each address opens in Maps. Start with the five routes below, or see it all on the 3D night map. They begin with coffee and music and end on a dancefloor, mostly without paying for entry.

How to spend each day

Free meansADE tagged it Free Festival or Free A&C. A dashed stamp means the organiser says free entry in their own text, but ADE did not tag it.

Free still fills upThe free parties at Blend XL, PRIK and Bears are small bars. Come at the start time, not two hours later.

Getting aroundWestergas, the centre and De Pijp are all bikeable in 15 to 20 minutes. The ferries behind Centraal to Noord and NDSM are free.

Open all week (installations, exhibitions, pop-ups)

Unofficial. A free fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Amsterdam Dance Event. Late events that start after midnight are listed under the night they belong to.

Source: the official ADE program filter (amsterdam-dance-event.nl, sections Festival, Arts & Culture and Friends, 21 to 25 October), pulled 19 Oct 2026 with every event detail page. Conference sessions and the ADE Pro program are left out. Districts come from geocoding each address with PDOK and matching it to the Gemeente Amsterdam stadsdelen map. Venues outside the city say so. Two event pages failed to load and show without address or description.

ADE does not publish ticket prices, so paid events show no price. Lineups change and parties sell out; check the event page before you go. Event links open the ADE page, and addresses open Google Maps.
